Project Objective

import difflib print("Welcome to chatbot and type 'exit' to quit") data = { "hii": "Hii Welcome to chatbot, what do you want from me:", "how you are able to answer me questions": "I have a data from which i answer your questions.", "in which language you are made": "I am Made in Python", "what is the nearest landmark": "The nearest landmark is Vaishnodevi Circle on the SG Highway.", "what courses do you offer": "We offer B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, and Ph.D. programs across various engineering, management...", "what are the btech branches available": "Our B.Tech branches include CSE, CSE (AI & ML), ICT, Civil & Infrastructure, Mechanical...", "what are the main faculties or schools": "The university operates primarily through the Faculty of engineering & Technology (FEST)...", "what mba specializations are there": "We offer MBAs in Infrastructure Management, Energy Management, Transportation, Urban...", "how are the placements": "Placements are strong due to our backing by the Adani Group. Average package is around 5 to 7.5 Lakhs...", "what is the highest placement package": "The highest package usually ranges between 14 to 15 Lakhs per annum..." } questions = list(data.keys()) while True: user = input("\nYou: ").strip().lower() if user == 'exit': print("Bot: Bye") break matches = difflib.get_close_matches(user, questions, n=1, cutoff=0.6) if matches: best_match = matches[0] print("Bot:", data[best_match]) else: print("Bot: I don't have answer about it in my knowledge.")

ENGINEERING GAP ANALYSIS

Limitations of the Initial difflib Approach

Why simple string proximity fails for real-world higher education guidance

Natural Language Bottleneck

1. Fragility to Natural Language

Students rarely type pre-programmed sentences. Queries like "Which private university in Ahmedabad has the lowest B.Tech fees?" fail completely because no exact key exists in the static dictionary.
Statutory Rule Void

2. Zero State Admission Domain Logic

Cannot compute ACPC merit marks (50% Board PR + 50% GUJCET PR), evaluate ACPUGMEC medical quotas, or apply Gujarat Government scholarships (MYSY, Kanya Kelavani Niti, TFWS).
Semantic Skew

3. High False Positive Rate

difflib calculates character n-gram proximity without understanding semantics. Queries asking for "medical colleges" often matched completely unrelated fields due to common English words.
Storage Constraint

4. Unscalable Knowledge Base

A static dictionary cannot scale to cover 55+ accredited Gujarat universities, multi-tiered fee structures (Govt vs GMERS vs SFI), round-wise cutoffs, and reservation criteria.
